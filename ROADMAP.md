# Roadmap

A rough plan for Trend Detective during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (14 and 15 November 2026).
Nothing here is final: we will adjust it as we learn what works. Crossed out items are done.

## Getting started
* ~~Gather the NASA datasets listed in the README into one consistent monthly format.~~
* ~~Agree on the statistical methods for trends and for links between quantities.~~
* ~~Sketch the interface: how a person picks a quantity and a place.~~

## Exploring
* ~~Trends for a territory: rate of change, uncertainty, significance.~~
* ~~Ways to tell a robust trend from a fragile one.~~
* ~~Maps of where the change is significant.~~
* ~~Links between quantities beyond a shared trend.~~
* ~~Whether continuing a trend a few years ahead can be done honestly, and where.~~
* ~~Checking our numbers against NASA's own tools.~~
* ~~An exhaustive search of links between quantities, confirmed on data the search did not see.~~
* ~~Chains of links, where one quantity affects a second through a third.~~
* ~~Satellite imagery next to the numbers, so a trend can be seen as well as measured.~~
* ~~Regions defined by geography: deserts, mountains, plateaus, seas, large cities.~~
* ~~Terrain types from MODIS land cover, to look only at the forests, croplands or deserts of a territory.~~
* ~~The atmosphere by height: temperature, humidity and ozone at pressure levels, and a trend-by-height view.~~

## Next
* ~~A simple mode for people without a background in statistics.~~
* ~~Separate views for outlooks and for links between several quantities.~~
* A layout that works on phones and on slower laptops.
* The presentation and a short demo for the judges.
* ~~Publishing the interface code in this repository.~~

## Open questions
* How simple can the interface be without losing the substance?
* Which quantities and regions make the clearest stories for the demo?
* How to show uncertainty so that it is understood, not ignored?
* What to leave out of the final version.

## Later
* More datasets and more regions.
* The exhaustive search of links for the new levels, regions and terrain types.
* Opening the tool and its data processing to others.
* Documentation.
