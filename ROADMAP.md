# Roadmap

A rough plan for Trend Detective during the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (14 and 15 November 2026).
Nothing here is final: we will adjust it as we learn what works.

## Getting started
* Gather the NASA datasets listed in the README into one consistent monthly format.
* Agree on the statistical methods for trends and for links between quantities.
* Sketch the interface: how a person picks a quantity and a place.

## Exploring
* Trends for a territory: rate of change, uncertainty, significance.
* Ways to tell a robust trend from a fragile one.
* Maps of where the change is significant.
* Links between quantities beyond a shared trend.
* Whether continuing a trend a few years ahead can be done honestly, and where.
* Checking our numbers against NASA's own tools.

## Open questions
* How simple can the interface be without losing the substance?
* Which quantities and regions make the clearest stories for the demo?
* How to show uncertainty so that it is understood, not ignored?
* What to leave out of the final version.

## Later
* More datasets and more regions.
* Opening the tool and its data processing to others.
* Documentation.
